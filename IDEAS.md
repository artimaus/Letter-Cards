## Match the Sound (megaphone cards) — built

Mockup: https://claude.ai/artifact/YZZKQL9GHwqrBQba6C7PED

- One big letter card at the top (with a dashed drop outline) and megaphone cards below, no letters on them.
- Voice asks "Which sound goes with… B?" (letter name).
- Tapping a megaphone plays its sound; it lifts and shows sound waves, then gets a "heard" tick.
- **Play before drag:** a megaphone can't be dragged until it has been played at least once.
- Drag a heard megaphone onto the letter. Dropping anywhere in the top half counts; a short or
  wobbly drag springs back and replays the sound.
- Wrong drop: springs back, "That one says mmm… Which sound goes with B?". 1st miss fades that
  megaphone; 2nd miss pulses the right one (same help as the other games).
- Right drop: megaphone flies into the card, card pops, "B says buh", occasional cheer, star.
- Look-alike sounds (b/d/p, m/n, f/v/th, s/z/sh) never share a board. Targets rotate evenly
  through the active sounds; finds count toward "sounds he knows".
- Question repeats after 12 s with no tap (up to 3 times).
- Banner: "Match the Sound", indigo, megaphone icon.
- New recording: "Which sound goes with…".
- Levels: 3 megaphones at levels 9–10, 4 megaphones at levels 11–12.
- Fallback if dragging is too fiddly: a grown-up setting for "tap a megaphone twice to choose".
