# Letter Hunt – ideas backlog

Ideas to discuss before building. Nothing here is in the game yet.

## Sound match (megaphone cards)

- Show one big letter card and three "megaphone" cards.
- Tapping a megaphone card plays a sound (no letter on it).
- The child drags the megaphone whose sound matches the letter onto the letter.
- Reverses "Find the sound": hear several sounds and pick the one that fits the letter,
  instead of hearing one sound and picking the letter.

Open questions to hash out:
- Drag vs. tap-to-select for a 3-year-old (dragging is harder at this age; maybe accept both).
- Does the letter say its name when the round starts, or stay silent?
- Wrong drop: megaphone springs back and replays its sound? Fade out a wrong option after a miss?
- Which levels it belongs to, and whether it grows to 4 megaphones.
- Distractor sounds should avoid confusable pairs (e.g. /b/ vs /d/, /m/ vs /n/) at first.
